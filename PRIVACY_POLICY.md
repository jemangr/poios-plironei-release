# Privacy Policy — Ποιος Πληρώνει; (`poios-plironei`)

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Developer:** Giannis Oikonomakis ([jemangr.app](https://jemangr.app/el/))

## 100% On-Device & Zero Data Collection

**Ποιος Πληρώνει;** runs completely locally on your Android device or web browser.

* **No Account & No Servers:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, touch coordinates, or generated images on external servers.
* **No Ads, Analytics, or Trackers:** Zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies are included.
* **Local Preferences Only:** Settings (sound mute, haptics toggle, fake-outs toggle, last selected mode, custom bet text, and last update check timestamp) are stored strictly in `localStorage` on your device.
* **Optional Update Check:** When checking for a new version (automatically every 6+ hours or manually in Settings), the app only performs a read-only HTTPS GET request to GitHub (`version.json` / latest release metadata) without sending any personal identifiers.
